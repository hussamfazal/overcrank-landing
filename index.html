Which cameras work with Overcrank?

Overcrank is built and hardware-tested for GoPro MISSION 1 Pro. It also supports HERO9 Black and later through the Open GoPro API; exact settings depend on the camera and firmware.

Do I need my iPhone with me?

Not for core Watch controls. Apple Watch talks directly to the camera over Bluetooth. iPhone adds Live Monitor, media tools, preset building, Multi-Cam, and the framing preview relayed to Watch.

What devices and software do I need?

An iPhone on iOS 17 or later and an Apple Watch on watchOS 10 or later. You can explore every screen in Demo Mode before connecting a camera.

What data does Overcrank collect?

None. There are no accounts, analytics, or servers. Camera traffic stays between your devices and camera, and Health data never leaves your device.
